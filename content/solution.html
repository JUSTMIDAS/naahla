Submersible Pump

Naahla provides complete water solutions that combine renewable energy, advanced purification, and efficient water delivery. Every system is built for long-term performance, simple operation, and proven results. Whether you are a household, a farm, or a community, our solutions are designed to give you clean water every day of the year.

Our submersible pump use the power of the sun to draw water from wells, rivers, or reservoirs. This provides a steady, dependable supply of water without relying on grid electricity.

  • Engineered for high efficiency and consistent performance.
  • Suitable for rural, urban, and off-grid environments.
  • Requires minimal maintenance.

How It Works 


Step One: Capture and Pump

Solar panel powers the submersible pump, drawing water from a well, borehole, or underground source.


Step Two: Purify and Protect

Water flows through a purification unit to remove contaminants.


Step Three: Deliver and Use

Water distributed to taps, storage tanks, or smart irrigation systems.


Step Four: Monitor and Maintain

Optional monitoring system checks performance and alerts when maintenance is needed.
