Naahla began with a lived reality that millions still face today. Growing up in Africa, our CEO, Mwende Mbevi, watched her grandmother walk to the river every day to collect water. That journey was not only heavy in weight but also heavy in cost. It took time, energy, and opportunity away from the family.

This reality is still present in communities across Africa and in other parts of the world where water scarcity and contamination disrupt daily life. Naahla was founded to change that reality through solutions that are sustainable, affordable, and built to last.

Mwende Mbevi
​Chief Executive Officer and Founder

Our Mission

To make clean water accessible, affordable, and sustainable for homes, farms, and communities worldwide.

Our Vision

A world where no one wakes up to an empty faucet and where water scarcity is a challenge of the past.

Why We Exist

We believe that access to clean water changes everything. It improves health, strengthens livelihoods, and creates new opportunities. Naahla exists to make those changes a reality, starting with one home, one farm, and one community at a time.
